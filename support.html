Reporting Safety Concerns

Your safety is our priority.

If you encounter a user who is:

  • Impersonating someone else
  • Harassing or threatening others
  • Attempting to scam users
  • Sharing inappropriate or illegal content
  • Violating our Community Guidelines

Please report them using the in-app reporting feature whenever possible. If you cannot access the app, email us at trueviewdating@gmail.com with as much detail as possible.

Where appropriate, include:

  • The user's display name
  • Screenshots (if available)
  • A description of what happened
  • The date and time of the incident
