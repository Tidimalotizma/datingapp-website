Community Guidelines

Discover the standards and expectations that help us maintain a safe, respectful, and welcoming community for all members.

Effective Date

16 July 2026

Last Updated

16 July 2026

Welcome to TrueViewDating

Our mission is simple:

Real Videos. Real People. Real Connections.

These Community Guidelines are designed to help create a respectful, authentic, and safe environment for everyone. By using TrueViewDating, you agree to follow these guidelines.

1. Be Respectful

Treat every member with kindness and respect.

We do not tolerate:

  • Harassment or bullying
  • Threats or intimidation
  • Hate speech
  • Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, age, gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation
  • Personal attacks or abusive language

Respect that every user has the right to say no.

2. Be Authentic

TrueViewDating is built around authenticity.

You must:

  • Use your own identity.
  • Upload your own photos and videos.
  • Provide truthful profile information.
  • Complete face verification honestly if you choose to use it.

Do not:

  • Create fake profiles.
  • Pretend to be someone else.
  • Use AI-generated or manipulated media to deceive others.
  • Share misleading information about yourself.

3. Keep the Community Safe

Your safety and the safety of others matter.

Never:

  • Attempt to scam other users.
  • Request banking details or passwords.
  • Encourage users to send money.
  • Promote investment scams, cryptocurrency scams, or pyramid schemes.
  • Share malicious software or suspicious links.

If someone asks you for money or personal financial information, report them immediately.

4. Appropriate Content

Your profile, photos, videos, messages, and calls should remain appropriate for a dating platform.

Do not post or share:

  • Nudity or sexually explicit content.
  • Pornographic material.
  • Graphic violence.
  • Illegal content.
  • Content involving minors.
  • Content encouraging self-harm or criminal activity.

We may remove content that violates these guidelines without notice.

5. Respect Privacy

Respect the privacy of everyone on TrueViewDating.

Do not:

  • Share another person's private information without permission.
  • Publish someone's phone number, address, workplace, or identification documents.
  • Record, screenshot, or distribute private conversations, voice calls, or video calls without permission, except where permitted by applicable law.

6. Communicate Responsibly

Messaging, voice calls, and video calls should be used responsibly.

Do not:

  • Send repeated unwanted messages.
  • Use sexually aggressive language.
  • Pressure anyone into meeting in person.
  • Threaten or manipulate another user.
  • Send spam or advertisements.

Consent and mutual respect are essential in every interaction.

7. Meeting in Person

If you decide to meet someone you've met on TrueViewDating:

  • Meet in a public place.
  • Tell a trusted friend or family member where you are going.
  • Arrange your own transportation.
  • Avoid sharing your home address too soon.
  • Trust your instincts—if something feels wrong, leave immediately.

Your personal safety always comes first.

8. Reporting Violations

Help us keep the community safe by reporting users who violate these guidelines.

You should report:

  • Fake profiles
  • Harassment
  • Hate speech
  • Scams
  • Impersonation
  • Inappropriate content
  • Underage users
  • Any behaviour that makes you feel unsafe

Reports are reviewed by our moderation team, and appropriate action will be taken.

9. Enforcement

Depending on the severity of a violation, we may:

  • Remove content.
  • Issue a warning.
  • Temporarily restrict account features.
  • Suspend an account.
  • Permanently ban an account.
  • Report illegal activity to the appropriate authorities where required by law.

Repeated violations may result in permanent removal from TrueViewDating.

10. Zero-Tolerance Policy

TrueViewDating has zero tolerance for:

  • Child sexual exploitation or abuse
  • Human trafficking
  • Terrorist or extremist content
  • Blackmail or extortion
  • Fraud and financial scams
  • Impersonation for malicious purposes
  • Sharing intimate images without consent
  • Any illegal activity

Accounts engaging in these activities will be permanently removed and may be reported to law enforcement.

11. Help Us Build a Better Community

Every member contributes to the TrueViewDating community.

By being respectful, honest, and considerate, you help create an environment where meaningful relationships can grow.

Thank you for helping make TrueViewDating a place for Real Videos. Real People. Real Connections.
